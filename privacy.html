Privacy & Policy

At AYZAN Skincare, your privacy and data security are treated with the highest dignity. Learn how we collect, safeguard, and responsibly process your personal information across Bangladesh.

01 Our Privacy Commitment

At AYZAN Skincare (“ayzan.me”, “we”, “our”), we believe that luxury skincare extends beyond physical bottles into how we honor our customers’ trust. We are committed to transparency, ethical data governance, and strict compliance with the laws of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website, register an account, utilize our AI Skin Advisor, or place orders for delivery across Bangladesh.

🔒 256-Bit SSL Encryption

Every session and checkout interaction is secured under enterprise SSL certificates.

🚫 Zero Data Selling

We will never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your contact info with third-party advertisers.

💳 Zero Financial Storage

We never collect, see, or store your bKash/Nagad PINs or credit card OTP credentials.

02 Information We Collect

To deliver an authentic, tailored skincare experience and execute nationwide shipments, we collect only necessary information:

  • Contact & Identity Details: Your full name (e.g. Rain), contact mobile number (used strictly for courier dispatch verification), and email address.
  • Delivery Information: Physical delivery address including Thana, Area, District, and postal instructions across Bangladesh.
  • Skin Profile Preferences (Optional): Skin type (oily, dry, combination, sensitive) and primary concerns entered during consultation or account creation, helping tailor personalized recommendations.
  • Order History & Transaction Records: Purchased formulation titles, invoice amounts in BDT, delivery zone, and MFS Transaction IDs (TrxID) for payment reconciliation.

03 How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To pack, dispatch, and track your skincare orders across our 64-district delivery network.
  • To transmit order status confirmations, parcel tracking IDs, and courier handover alerts via SMS and email.
  • To maintain your AYZAN VIP Membership dashboard, granting fast 1-click reordering and access to member privileges.
  • To respond to your inquiries through our concierge desk at her.skincare@ayzan.me.

04 Absolute Protection of Payment Credentials

Payment security is of paramount importance. When paying via bKash, Nagad, or Rocket:

Crucial Guarantee: You conduct transactions directly within your own banking app or MFS dialer. We only receive a transaction confirmation reference number (TrxID) and the associated amount. AYZAN personnel will NEVER ask for your MFS PIN, verification OTP, or card CVV.

05 Courier & Logistics Partner Disclosures

To deliver your parcel directly to your home, office, or university, we share your Name, Mobile Number, and Delivery Address exclusively with our licensed third-party courier services in Bangladesh (such as Steadfast Courier and Pathao Express).

These courier partners are legally prohibited from utilizing your contact details for any purpose other than executing parcel delivery.

06 Data Security & Storage Standards

We deploy robust administrative, electronic, and physical safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access, accidental alteration, or disclosure of customer records. All cloud data storage is encrypted at rest and in transit.

07 Cookies & Browser Storage

Our website utilizes local storage technologies strictly for functional shopping utilities:

  • Remembering items in your Shopping Bag while navigating between pages.
  • Saving your curated Wishlist so you can easily review saved formulations on your next visit.
  • Maintaining your active VIP login session so you do not have to re-enter credentials upon every visit.

We do not operate intrusive third-party cross-site advertising trackers.

08 Your Customer Rights

As a valued customer of AYZAN Skincare, you possess full ownership over your data:

  • Access & Correction: You may review and update your saved delivery address and skin profile at any time in your Account Dashboard.
  • Erasure: You have the right to request full deletion of your customer account and associated records by emailing our privacy team.
  • Opt-Out: You may unsubscribe from VIP seasonal drop newsletters at any time with a single click.

09 Data Retention Period

We retain transaction histories and order records only for as long as required by Bangladesh tax, auditing, and e-commerce compliance regulations, after which data is safely anonymized or permanently deleted.
